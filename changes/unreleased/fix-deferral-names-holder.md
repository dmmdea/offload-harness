### Fixed - a busy deferral names who holds the cards and when to come back, and a media door's success answer states the cards it ran on and the host RAM it declared

- **What was wrong.** The vision gate deferred with `gpu busy: generation job holds the lock (696s)` when the lease on the cards its seat is pinned to stayed held for the whole wait. A caller who saw one card held by its own card-scoped lease and another card free read that as a gate blind to card scope; the gate is card-scoped, the seat simply sits on two cards and one was held, and the sentence said neither. The media doors' busy deferral (`held by a media-class lease (Ns, reason "...")`) named no epoch, group, cards or term end, and the result carried no structured holder. A finished video, animate or music answer did not say which cards it ran on, and no media answer said the host RAM its call declared for its lease.
- **What it does.** A busy deferral that has a lease behind it carries `{"holders":[{epoch, class, reason, group, held_sec, remaining_sec, expires_at, devices}], "retry_after_sec": N}` in its data (`core.LaneHolder` grew `group`, `held_sec` and `expires_at`; the lane probe's holders carry them too), and its reason names the lease's epoch, group and cards and when its term ends. `retry_after_sec` is the lease's remaining term and is left out when it declared none or it has passed. The vision gate now says `gpu busy: the vision seat <model> sits on cards <id>, <id>; a media-class lease (epoch 1906, group <g>, cards <id>, reason "...") has held them for 696s and its term ends in 512s` (the seat's cards are left out when they cannot be said), keeping the `gpu busy:` prefix, and `video_watch` carries the holders block once on its all-windows-deferred answer. The video, animate and music answers add `devices` when the call held the card-only pair, and every media answer whose call declared host RAM adds `host_ram_declared_gib` (a `0` included) and `host_ram_commit_declared_gib` when a commit was stated. All additive: a caller that ignores the new fields sees the same answer.
- **Checks.** The vision-gate case in `TestVisionGPULockHeldDefersWithDistinctReason` pins the new sentence and the holders block; two busy-answer checks that asserted no data at all now assert no `waiter_token`. Not done: the sd.cpp video and audio lanes (`runIGPU`), run-graph and the image batch do not state the host RAM yet.
