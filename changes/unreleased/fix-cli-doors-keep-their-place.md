### Fixed - the CLI media verbs can keep their place in line, so a caller that waited is no longer passed by one that asks at once

- **What was wrong.** A media call that cannot get its card within `gpu_wait_ms` (90 s) answers deferred, and the caller sleeps and asks again. The harness keeps a place in line for that (a `waiter_token`, resumed with its original arrival time), but only for a door that can hand the token back: the MCP tools did, the CLI verbs did not, and had no flag to send it. A caller of `generate-video` that had asked for eight minutes was passed three times by another caller whose previous call had just returned and who asked at once; whoever asked first after a release won. The answers it read were `gpu_busy` with `holders[]` and `retry_after_sec`, which leave no place at all. That was true of every shape of call (one card, two cards of a three-card machine, the whole node): the request of a CLI verb was never marked resumable, so no admission path left a place for it.
- **What it does.** `generate-video`, `animate-character`, `generate-audio` and a single `edit-image-generative` mark their request resumable and take `--waiter-token <token>`. A call that waits out its window now answers `gpu_queued` with the token (`result.waiter_token` under `--json`; the plain-text form ends with `To keep your place in line, run the same command again with --waiter-token <token>`), and the same command with the flag resumes the place with the arrival time it left with. The queued answer also carries `holders[]` and `retry_after_sec`, worked out as a busy answer works them, so a caller that paced its re-ask by them keeps doing so. A token that is not asked for again stops holding the line back after 30 s. Not changed: `generate-image` and `run-graph` (they block on the ticket queue on a box with a fleet), `upscale-image`, `inpaint`, `edit-image` and the batches, which still answer `gpu_busy`.
- **Checks.** One test drives a door against a held card and reads the token, the holder and the retry figure in the queued answer (`TestTheQueuedAnswerStillNamesTheHolderAndWhenToComeBack`); the existing queued-answer and resume tests pass unchanged.
