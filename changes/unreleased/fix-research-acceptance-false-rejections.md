### Fixed - the research lane stops failing digests that are right: a digest that quotes its page, an honest "none", and a page with no content

- **What was wrong.** In the 30 days to 2026-11-12, 346 of the 356 `failed_verification` rows with a job id were research pages, and three checks the lane writes itself were failing answers that were right. (1) The page-word tripwire (`docanchor`) takes twenty-four prose words of the page that the goal does not contain and asks the loop's final prose to restate one; a digest of a narrow goal restates the goal's words and the page's figures, so 282 digests failed it, and the digests of 187 of them hold strings found on the page word for word. (2) A caller's schema that lists an array in `required` got `min_items:<first required array>:1`, which failed the digest the goal asks for when the page has no such content (an empty list and a sentence that says so): 70 digests failed it, and 64 of those failed nothing else. (3) A page with no content (a title, a cookie gate, a login wall) was dispatched as a full run: 16 of the failures and 109 of the passes were pages under 150 characters. Each acceptance-only failure discarded a whole run and was not retried.
- **What changed (1).** `delegate.DigestGrounded` excuses a failed fingerprint, in the same place and for the same reasons as the honest-absence excuse (so the merge gate, the quarantine and the retry rule read one answer), when the decoded digest holds one string of at least 60 characters, or two different strings of at least 15, found in the contract's own context documents with the quote check's lookup (case, spacing, quote marks and dashes set aside). A string the goal also contains counts for nothing, so the goal echoed back, the page's title and URL and sentences pasted into the goal cannot ground a digest. It only excuses: the node that wrote the digest is neither struck nor counted as passing.
- **What changed (2).** `research.Build` derives `nonempty:<f1>|<f2>|...` over the caller's required fields (a value in any one of them) in place of the item count, for a schema that lists an array in `required` and for no other. It fails a digest silent in every required field, and can only fail what the item count failed; a caller who wants items writes `min_items:` themselves.
- **What changed (3).** `research.Build` refuses an HTML page whose text is under `research.MinPageRunes` (150 characters) before any seat is spent on it: a skipped source with `error_kind` `empty_page`. JSON, plain text, feeds and subtitle tracks are never refused by length. A skipped source writes no ledger row, as for every other skipped source; `bad_input` is the class a row would carry.
- **Thresholds, and the data behind each.** The 60 and 15 characters and the two strings come from a grid of rules scored on the 282 fingerprint failures and on pairs of each of the 4,968 digests of those 30 days with three pages of other sites and up to three of the same site: a digest paired with an unrelated page of another site is grounded in 7 of 14,904 pairs (0.05 %), against 0.2 to 1.2 % when short figures also count; 11 % of same-site pairs are grounded (shared footers, price tables, one page under two addresses), which is why the rule is an excuse for a digest written from this page and not a proof against its own site. The 150 characters stop where nearly all that is refused is a shell: of the 109 passing pages that were not JSON, a handful were short real text.
- **Check.** An offline replay of the saved pages and answers through `research.Build` and the merge gate (`delegate.EvalAcceptanceGraded`), read-only on the ledger and the delegation log. The original code reproduces every saved verdict (356 failed rows fail, 4,622 passed rows pass). With the change: of the 356, 167 pass (125 of the 282 fingerprint failures by quoting the page, 42 of the 64 item-count failures), 16 are refused as pages with no content, 173 still fail (10 are not research); of the 4,622 passes, 4,513 still pass, 109 are refused as pages with no content and none fails. Planted negatives from real rows: a digest paired with another site's page and rejected by the fingerprint stays rejected (0 of 200 newly accepted by the grounding rule), digests whose strings were altered so they are not on the page stay rejected (0 of 176), a silent empty digest fails (0 of 200 with a closing word), the goal echoed back fails (0 of 100). The known limit, as before and now for caller schemas too: a seat that says "none" for a page that holds the items is believed (200 of 200 planted pass the fingerprint through the honest-absence excuse; 24 of them, under a caller's schema, were failed by the item count before). One test drives the lane's public path with a page and digest per case.
- **Docs.** `docs/systems/mcp-server.md` (research acceptance, the derived check, skipped sources). The two tests that pinned the item-count check now pin the any-of check.
