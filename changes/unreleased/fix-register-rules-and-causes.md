### Fixed - the failure register files 27 more rows under the class that names them, and the four classes it had not investigated now carry their causes

- **What was wrong.** After the last release 30 failure rows still sat in `other` and 3 in `poll_deadline`, and four classes read "not investigated" (`failed_verification` 357 rows, `other`, `poll_deadline` 3, `stall_cold_load` 4). The rows in `other` were lane refusals and transport faults whose sentences or error classes no rule named. The 3 `poll_deadline` rows were not that class: each ends on a last poll error, so the node had stopped answering, not kept working past the allowance. One audio row was filed `failed_verification` though the audio was never judged (ffmpeg or ffprobe was missing).
- **Where each row now goes.** `no node that answered is called ...` (8 rows) is `node_busy`, the class the lane's own defer already files for new rows through its wire class. A dispatch whose connection could not be made (1), `N consecutive poll failures` (1), `rendered the job but fetching it failed` (1) and the 3 poll-deadline rows with a last poll error are `node_unreachable`. Error class `no_node_configured` (2) and the audio QA's missing-ffmpeg dead air (1) are `config`. `media convert: convert: in and out are required` (1) and error class `lint_errors` (1) are `bad_input`. `reasoning tier: low classify confidence ...` (3) is `abstention`. Compose's `check_failed` (5) is `failed_verification`.
- **Two readings that are a choice.** `rendered the job but fetching it failed` is `node_unreachable` (the fetch dropped), with `infrastructure` the other reading because the render itself succeeded. Compose `check_failed` is `failed_verification` (the lane's own checks of the composition failed), with `bad_input` the other reading because the caller wrote the composition. Both are one-line rule changes.
- **Left alone on purpose.** The browse door's `blocked` (4 rows) is proposed as `abstention` but its cause is not settled, so it has no rule; the 3 `generate_svg` rows with no reason, no reason code and no door cannot be traced. `other` is 7 rows.
- **How.** Each rule needs the writer's own fixed words. `media convert: convert: in and out are required` and `reasoning tier: low classify confidence ` are matched as whole sentences, not as the bare prefixes `media convert:` and `reasoning tier: `, because `media <op>: ` also wraps what ffmpeg reported and `reasoning tier: ` wraps other verifier wordings. Three new error classes are in `errClassCodes`; the dispatch, poll-failure and fetch sentences are in `legacyReasonCode`; the poll-deadline and audio rules are in `RefileStamped` and each needs both the old stamp and the writer's sentence, so no other row of those classes moves. The pin test `TestEveryCoreErrClassHasALedgerReasonCode` listed every error class but `no_node_configured`; it does now.
- **Causes.** `internal/ledger/rootcause.go` now holds the findings, with their counts and the day measured, and says where the evidence stops. `failed_verification` is a harness defect for the research lane's own checks: of 357 rows, 307 are those checks rejecting answers that were right or honest, of which 202 are established by comparing the digest to the page and 105 rest on the seat's own statement; 10 are seat misses, 4 a caller regex over-escaped, 1 a misfile, 35 undetermined. `poll_deadline` is an upstream node that stopped answering (why is not settled). `stall_cold_load` is one placement and filing defect on 2026-11-04: the placer put a long-context subtask on a seat that shares the pair's cards with the pool seat, and the monitor filed the wait behind the swap as a cold-load stall. `other` is a bundle of misfiled sentences, not one cause. The follow-ups it names (stop deriving the `min_items` check, count the call's own siblings in `pairLong`, record the answer in the ledger, stop stamping a poll deadline with a last poll error as `poll_deadline` at the writer) are not made here.
- **Docs.** The catalogue copy and the rule lists on `docs/systems/failure-register.md` match the code; `causes_not_investigated` is empty in the window.
- **Check.** The built binary and the installed one, both read-only on the real ledger, `ledger --failures --since 30`: 2,566 failure rows of 8,785 job rows in both, the sum over classes unchanged. `other` 30 to 7, `poll_deadline` 3 to 0, `node_busy` 704 to 712, `node_unreachable` 3 to 9, `failed_verification` 357 to 361, `abstention` 221 to 224, `config` 32 to 35, `bad_input` 2 to 4; every other class is unchanged. `causes_not_investigated` went from `failed_verification`, `other`, `poll_deadline`, `stall_cold_load` to none. Two further rows of the `no_node_configured` class are measurement runs the register leaves out.
