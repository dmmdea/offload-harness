### Added - `docs/systems/self-improvement.md`: how the harness improves itself on evidence, one page that maps the discipline, the failure register, the trace-corpus readout, the measurement gate and SHA-bound review evidence

- **The gap.** Four pieces of the evidence loop each have a page of their own, and nothing said how they fit: that a readout never decides, that only the measurement gate adopts, that a missing measurement is `null` and never 0, and which of the pieces is the one that can say "better". A reader met `BELOW_GATE` in the trace-corpus report and could take it for an ADOPT.
- **What the page holds.** The discipline (measure, gate, adopt or keep the incumbent; quality is the only gate and no pass rule carries a wall-time term), then one section per part that is on main, each naming the verb or tool that runs it, what it answers, the rules that keep it honest, and the code and the page that own the detail: `local-offload ledger --failures` (the six closed kinds, refused attempts set aside, unparsed lines and a missing ledger counted, the cause and fix kept as a person's), `local-offload trace-corpus` (harvest, readout, freeze, check; the pager re-fetch rate against its 10 % gate; the compaction pass count; unrecorded reads `null`), `local-offload gate` (freeze, check, verdict, schema, selftest; the rules in short; the judge receipt), and `offload_review_diff` (the diff fingerprint, the optional base and head commits echoed and never resolved, gates first, the five outcomes with `NOT_RUN` never a PASS, the second fingerprint check, the durable trace beside the ledger). It also lists the entry points, the invariants, the privacy notes, the tests and the pitfalls.
- **Two parts marked "not on main yet".** The evidence-preserving reducer rung and the recipe store with its audit verb each get one short paragraph: proposed, what it will do in one sentence, and that it stays off until its own gate measurement passes. Their code is not described because it is not merged.
