### Fixed - a card-only video call that falls back to the streaming estimate says why

- **What was wrong.** A video call that asked for the card-only placement (`videogen_placement: multicard`, `--wan-placement multicard`) and did not get it was declared the ordinary streaming set with no word about the placement. Seen on a node with two 15.0 GiB cards, `videogen_family` `ltx25` and a stray top-level `videogen_wan_resident_gb: 2`: the door printed only `34.3 GiB host RAM (estimate: ltx25: unet 20.0 + text encoder 14.3 = 34.3 GiB does not fit the 15.0 GiB card (14.0 GiB usable), so the weights stream from RAM)` and deferred. Nothing said the card-only placement had been asked for and refused, or that the 2 GiB resident share had pushed 18 GiB of the 20 GiB DiT onto the storage card, which holds 14 GiB usable. Without that key the same call is admitted card-only (the family's own share is 9 GiB).
- **What it does.** Every branch where the Wan 2.2, LTX-2.5 or WAN-Animate-2 card-only sizing falls back to the ordinary set now appends the reason to the declaring line: the placement was asked for, the recipe did not fit the two leased cards, each card's peak against the room it has (the compute card against the whole larger card, the storage card against what the smaller one has after the runner's reserve), and, when the call's resident share is not the family's own, that share and its value (`videogen_wan_resident_gb`, the family's `wan_resident_gb` or `--wan-resident-gb`) and how much of the DiT it leaves on the storage card. A lease that does not hold exactly two cards, or a DiT file that cannot be sized, says that instead. The figure declared is unchanged.
- **Checks.** `TestTheResidentShareIsReadFromTheCall` carries the LTX-2.5 case above and the Wan one; no new test.
