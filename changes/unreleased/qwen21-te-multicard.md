### Added - qwen-image-2.1 bf16 runs pooled on two cards: compute-first launch, its 16.33 GiB text encoder split over both cards and freed before the sampler, measured at 2048x2048

- **What it does.** The `qwen-image-2.1/bf16` row of `render/multicard-recipes.json` (and its Go mirror in `internal/mediacap/multicard.go`) is no longer held back, and it is now `measured`, so it declares 0 GiB of host RAM on the lease. Its text encoder loads through `OffloadCLIPLoaderDisTorch2`, a new node of the harness's own pack (`render/comfyui-ext/nodes/offload_multicard`), named by the new `distorch2-host` clip loader kind. The node runs ComfyUI-MultiGPU's `CLIPLoaderDisTorch2MultiGPU` with ComfyUI's text-encoder initial device answered with the CPU for that one load, then places the encoder at once through DisTorch2's own load path. It stops with `OFFLOAD-TE-HOSTWEIGHT` if more than 64 MiB of weights are left in host RAM. Both 2.1 rows carry `"cardOrder": "compute-first"`. Their pair instance starts with the compute card listed first (`cuda:0` computes, `cuda:1` stores, instance key `mcc-...`, the LTX-2.5 order), and `rowDevices` in `render/multicard-recipes.mjs` puts every loader on the matching device. The 2.1 multicard graph routes its conditioning through `OffloadFreeAfterEncode`, which frees the encoder from both cards before the DiT loads. `qwen-image-2.1` joins the families with a launchable image recipe (a 2048x2048 canvas cap, the family default), and `imagegen_placement: multicard` no longer warns for it. The int8 convrot row stays held back: no card-only run of it on the two non-display cards has been made.
- **Why.** Under `--gpu-only` ComfyUI builds the whole text encoder on one card before the DisTorch2 allocation applies, so the stock loader ran out of memory 1.4 s in (14.10 GiB allocated). With the encoder fixed, a storage-first render then aborted at the first sampler step (`Fatal Python error: Aborted`, 406 s in). The 2.1 model calls comfy_kitchen fused kernels, which run on `cuda:0` only, and ComfyUI-MultiGPU CPU-staged tensors from `cuda:1` to `cuda:0` through host RAM (upstream issue 220). The row's old evidence line also called the encoder's share 13.3 GiB. That is the bf16 DiT file's size; the encoder is 16.33 GiB, and the row says so.
- **Check.** One real render on the 5070 Ti (compute) and a 5060 Ti (storage), with 0 GiB host RAM declared on the lease: 2048x2048, 40 steps, 280.0 s at 5.57 s per step.
  - The encoder placed in 7.3 s (`cuda:0` 8.33 GiB, `cuda:1` 8.00 GiB, 0 on the CPU). The release freed both cards in 0.8 s (14.48 and 14.80 GiB free).
  - The DiT was built empty and landed 8 GiB on `cuda:0` and 5.25 GiB on `cuda:1`. Peaks were 10.90 GiB on the compute card and 8.12 GiB on the storage card.
  - Host free memory dipped only while weights streamed to the cards (64.6 and 75.0 GiB, from 83.9) and held at 81 GiB through sampling.
  - No CPU-staging line appeared in the console.
  - The image was read back at full size: coherent, sharp, on the prompt.
