### Added - the qwen-image-2.1 bf16 card-only recipe launches: a harness-owned loader builds its 16.33 GiB text encoder off the cards and splits it over both

- **What it does.** The `qwen-image-2.1/bf16` row of `render/multicard-recipes.json` (and its Go mirror in `internal/mediacap/multicard.go`) is no longer held back. Its text encoder loads through `OffloadCLIPLoaderDisTorch2`, a new node of the harness's own pack (`render/comfyui-ext/nodes/offload_multicard`), named by the new `distorch2-host` clip loader kind. The node runs ComfyUI-MultiGPU's `CLIPLoaderDisTorch2MultiGPU` with ComfyUI's text-encoder initial device answered with the CPU for that one load, then places the encoder at once through DisTorch2's own load path by `cuda:1,8gb;cuda:0,*`: about 8 GiB on the compute card, 8.33 GiB on the storage card, computing on the storage card. It stops with `OFFLOAD-TE-HOSTWEIGHT` if more than 64 MiB of weights are left in host RAM. `qwen-image-2.1` joins the families with a launchable image recipe (a 2048x2048 canvas cap, the family default, chosen and not measured), `imagegen_placement: multicard` no longer warns for it, and the int8 convrot row stays held back for its own reason (the int8 DiT on `cuda:1`, upstream issue 220) and names the same loader.
- **Why.** Under `--gpu-only` ComfyUI builds the whole text encoder on the text-encoder card before the DisTorch2 allocation applies, so the stock loader ran out of memory 1.4 s in (14.10 GiB allocated) on a 15.9 GiB card. The row's old evidence line also called the encoder's share 13.3 GiB; that is the bf16 DiT file's size, the encoder is 16.33 GiB, and the row says so. Host RAM is used only transiently, while the encoder is staged on its way to the cards at load time.
- **Check.** `node --test` on the recipe, builder, image-runner, canvas and 2.1 workflow tests; the node file parses; `go build ./...`, `go vet ./internal/pipeline` and the `mediacap`, `config`, `hostneed` and `mediaremote` tests. Not run on a GPU: the peaks (compute 11.0, storage 10.1 GiB) are arithmetic, the row's basis stays `predicted`. The first live render has to read where the encoder's bytes land on each card (the node's `[offload_multicard] text encoder ... placed` log line), how much host RAM the staging takes, and that the encoder has left the compute card before the DiT's share lands on it. Details: `docs/systems/media-generation.md`, "Card-only image and edit".
